A fitness brand built one week at a time, by a student who went from the mountains of Kyrgyzstan to the gyms of South Korea.

CAMP 10 m · Concept

Nobody teleports to the top.

ascend is about the climb, not the summit photo. Progress is slow, stepwise and earned. The brand looks the way the training feels: honest, steady and a little bit hard.

  1. 01

    The steps

    The left side climbs in equal steps. Each one is a week of training: same height, no shortcuts.

  2. 02

    Three steps, then the slope

    Like the program: learn the basics, build the habit, then the hard climb starts.

  3. 03

    The peak

    The summit sits off-centre. The top is never where you expect it, and it's always a bit further.

  4. 04

    The slope

    From the top, the climb looks like one clean line. That's what people see. The steps are what you did.

Roots

The Tian Shan mountains of Kyrgyzstan. Altitude, snow, rock and early sunrise give the brand its colors.

Discipline

"Held myself to a high standard." The brand never promises easy. It promises honest.

Proof

Numbers over hype. Weights, reps and weeks are shown in a monospaced type, like a training log.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of one step in the mark (a sixth of its height).

Minimum size

Mark: 16 px. Full logo: 96 px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

Name in text

Always lowercase, no full stop in running text: "join ascend", not "Join ASCEND."

Don't flip it. The steps always climb left to right.

Don't stretch or squash it.

No gradients, no glow, no drop shadows.

ASCEND

Don't set the name in capitals.

CAMP 66,100 m · Voice

Talk like a training partner

Direct, honest, a bit dry. Short sentences. Say the hard part out loud. Never hype, never shame.

Honest first

Say what it takes before what you'll get.

Show numbers

"60 × 8 in week 1, 75 × 8 in week 8" beats "insane gains".

Respect effort

Talk to people who show up. Don't mock the ones who haven't started.

This is us

  • No sugarcoating. This won't work if you don't.
  • Nobody gets a dream body in 8 weeks. Mine took 1.5 years.
  • Show up when you don't feel like it.
  • See progress, or get your money back.

Not us

  • Get shredded in 30 days!!!
  • The one secret trainers don't want you to know
  • Only weak people skip leg day
  • Limited offer, 90% off, ends tonight
